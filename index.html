Press Release

Flags USA Partners with the USO Through December 2026.

CAROL STREAM, IL — MAY 7, 2026 · Flags USA announced today a partnership with the USO and its mission of supporting service members and their families.

This collaboration reflects strong natural synergies between the USO and Flags USA. Both organizations share a deep dedication to patriotism, service, and meaningful ways for Americans to show their support for those who serve.

As part of the initiative, Flags USA customers will have the opportunity to give back at checkout by adding a $2.50 donation to their purchase, directly benefiting the USO's programs and services for active-duty troops and their families.

In addition, the USO will launch a dedicated fundraising campaign inviting supporters to contribute to its mission. To further amplify donor impact, Flags USA has committed to matching all donations made through the campaign, up to $25,000, doubling the generosity of contributors.

"We are grateful to Flags USA for supporting the USO's mission. This campaign will have a direct impact on the lives of our service members and their families around the globe." Ben Leslie · USO Chief Development Officer

"At Flags USA, the American flag has always represented far more than a product. The flag represents the people who have sacrificed to protect the freedoms we all share. Partnering with the USO allows us to support service members and their families during such an important moment in our country's history leading into America 250." Kim Dacka · CEO, Flags USA

The partnership reinforces Flags USA's commitment to family, service, and the American Spirit, during a celebratory year on the country's 250th birthday.

About the USO

The USO is the leading nonprofit dedicated to strengthening the well-being of the people serving in America's military and their families. Since 1941, the USO has stood beside service members throughout their military service through 260+ locations around the world, care package delivery, entertainment, and military spouse and youth programming. Visit USO.org.

About Flags USA

Founded in 1987, Flags USA began as a small, family-run business. Over 37 years later, the women-owned and family-run operation has grown to a 4,000 square foot warehouse with multiple mother-daughter teams. Every flag is proudly made in America.
